# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Event attendees using a shared booth on tablet, desktop, or mobile. They need to choose a New York scene, capture a usable selfie, and receive a memorable postcard-style result without learning a new interface or creating an account.

## Product Purpose

Turn a short event interaction into a polished creative keepsake. Success means an attendee can understand the flow at a glance, complete it confidently in about a minute, and trust that their photo remains private during this local prototype.

## Brand Personality

Polished, kinetic, magical. The experience should feel like a professional cloud-powered studio compressed into a playful, immediate booth interaction.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards: dense card grids, tiny controls, muted admin styling, configuration-heavy layouts, and interfaces that expose system complexity instead of hiding it.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next action unmistakable.
2. Turn waiting into visible creative progress.
3. Keep technical sophistication behind a simple attendee flow.
4. Use motion and personality to reward action, never to delay it.
5. Treat privacy and accessibility as part of the experience, not supporting copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain strong contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic status announcements, touch targets of at least 44 by 44 pixels, and complete reduced-motion support. Never rely on color alone to communicate progress, selection, success, or failure.
